One muscle group a day with a resistance tube, plus a food log that tracks protein, carbs and fat against your targets.

Warm up 5 minArm circles, bodyweight squats, light band pull-aparts.
EffortStop each set 1–3 reps short of failure, with clean form.
Tempo & rest2 s on the way back, 1 s squeeze. Rest 60–90 s between sets.
Progress weeklyHit the top of the rep range on every set? Step further from the anchor, shorten your grip, or switch to a heavier tube.

7-day plan

Each day trains one muscle group with extra sets, so it gets a full week to recover. Tick each exercise as you finish it; ticks clear at midnight. Tap an exercise name for its demo, setup and form cues. Every 6th week, do the same plan with a lighter tube to recover.
